FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us at (480) 555-0142.

How do Puurk payment plans work?

At checkout, choose to pay over time and pick from several plan lengths. You'll see your monthly amount, number of payments and total before you sign. Payments are drawn automatically from the card you add.

Do I have to leave this site to pay?

No. Checkout happens right here on our website. Puurk securely handles your plan agreement and card details, and Stripe processes the payments.

Is a consultation required before treatment?

For injectables and laser treatments, yes. We'll review your goals and medical history first. Consultations booked online are credited toward your first treatment.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule. Late cancellations may be subject to a $50 fee.

Can I combine treatments into one plan?

Yes. Our Signature Packages bundle complementary treatments at a better value, and the whole package can be spread across one payment plan.
